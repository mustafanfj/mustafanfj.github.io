Projects and funding

Research that left the lab: two funded agricultural systems and a health-tech startup.

CropSage

TwinView · R2C program, 100,000 AED

TwinView is a dual-view handheld imaging system that returns a diagnosis, its severity, the visual evidence and a recommended action in seconds. It won 100,000 AED from the R2C program. We are now commercializing it, shaping the product around the problems growers actually have, and validating it with farmers in greenhouses in Al Ain. I founded the project and lead its research.

Agri-Sensei

Make it in the Emirates 2026 · Silal research funding, 650,000 AED

Agri-Sensei is our plant disease detector. We took it to Make it in the Emirates 2026 with Dr. Mobeen Ur Rehman, met Silal there, and secured 650,000 AED in research funding for the Autonomous Systems Lab.

Dovvi

P2M 2025 · first place, 150,000 AED

Dovvi is a maternal-health wearable that tracks fetal movement and maternal vitals. As its co-founder and CTO, I won first place and 150,000 AED with the team at P2M 2025, UAEU Science and Innovation Park.

Why this work

I study Computer & AI Engineering at UAE University, graduating in May 2027, and I am looking for a master's place in computer vision.

Most of my work asks one question: can we trust what a vision system sees? I try to answer it with mechanisms rather than prompts: models grounded in visual evidence, weighted by how reliable they proved on held-out data, allowed to abstain, and small enough to run on the hardware the field allows.

I test these ideas where mistakes cost something: over flooded streets, where every byte on a weak drone link counts; on crop leaves, with a diagnosis a farmer in Al Ain can act on; and on coral reefs, where bleaching has to be judged from murky underwater images.

In 2026 that became seven papers, four as first author, with released code for each main system. This summer I joined the eBRAIN Lab at NYU Abu Dhabi to study why compressed perception models break down in fog and rain.
